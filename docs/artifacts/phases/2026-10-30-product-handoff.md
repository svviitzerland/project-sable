## Brief and scope

Original local attachment (not portable to another computer): `/Users/valent/.codex/attachments/1f09edfc-2621-42c1-ba31-5c392df25a45/Pasted text.txt`.

The brief describes Jude, evidence-grounded courtroom intelligence for lawyers in Indonesian criminal proceedings. Initial scenario: a simulated hearing with synthetic or authorized case documents/audio. The required product flow is:

1. Authenticated, case-isolated workspaces; upload PDF and other case material, retain original files and document/page provenance.
2. Speechmatics live transcription, timestamps, confidence, diarized voice IDs; do not infer courtroom identities from voices. Microphone and prepared audio support.
3. Meaningful claim extraction and case-filtered evidence retrieval using embeddings/pgvector plus keywords.
4. Bounded OpenRouter pipeline: claim extraction → evidence analyst → independent adversarial reviewer → synthesizer. Transcription must continue while statements are analyzed.
5. Progressive findings: Supported, Potential Inconsistency, Context Needed, Insufficient Evidence. Inspect exact passages, uncertainty, alternative explanations, verification steps, suggested questions, and review notes.
6. Indonesian legal discovery through Pasal.id, carefully distinguishing metadata/links from verified legal text.
7. Supabase PostgreSQL/pgvector and private Storage support; provider credentials only on the backend.
8. Evaluation using labeled examples: inconsistency precision/recall, citation validity, unsupported conclusions, latency, and transcription quality where a reference transcript exists.

No guilt, truthfulness, credibility, or judicial outcome determinations. No invented production/privacy promises, measured performance, commercial terms, or customer claims. Prosecutor/judge variants are future work.

## Decisions already made

- Next.js frontend + FastAPI backend retained.
- `/workspace` extends the existing forest green/ivory identity. Manrope for controls, restrained serif titles, transcript/findings/source inspector, case rail and explicit documents/references/session tabs.
- Local SQLite and private local files work without accounts from external providers. Supabase PostgreSQL and Storage are optional configured backends.
- Application-owned auth uses scrypt passwords, opaque hashed session tokens, HttpOnly/SameSite cookies, and owner checks. This is **not Supabase Auth**.
- The synthetic hearing uses explicitly labeled authored fixtures, never represented as AI output or speech recognition.
- Manual transcript entry without OpenRouter saves testimony and shows analysis unavailable; it never invents a finding.
- Live integrations are implemented but **not verified with real credentials**. No provider keys were configured during this work.
- Pasal REST returns discovery metadata/article links, not full legal text. It cannot be treated as substantive legal evidence.
- In-process tasks/events/hearing locks mean this MVP currently requires **one backend worker**. Restart marks interrupted work for retry. This is not a distributed/durable job system.
