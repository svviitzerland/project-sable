# 2026-11-02: Pricing page and simulated plans

## Scope

- Public pricing page at `/pricing` (Bahasa Indonesia primary, English), linked from the landing header, mobile menu, closing section and footer. Plans: Coba Gratis, Advokat Lite, Advokat Pro, Kantor Hukum (recommended, emphasized) and a Lembaga & LBH band with hour packs. Monthly/yearly toggle with yearly by default, animated prices (instant under reduced motion), per-hour anchors, hours translated into hearing sessions, trust strip, plan calculator, billing protections, payment methods, comparison table (collapsible on mobile) and FAQ accordion.
- Pricing logic in `apps/web/src/lib/pricing.ts` with unit tests in `tests/pricing.test.ts`. Scheme, unit economics and the gateway recommendation in [`docs/product/PRICING.md`](../product/PRICING.md).
- Simulated billing: migration `009_user_plans.sql` and matching SQLite columns, `GET`/`POST /api/v1/account/plan` (Zod contracts, instant switch, read-only usage metering), plan id in `/auth/me`, a confirm dialog on the pricing page for signed-in users, and plan plus usage meters with a "Change plan" link in the workspace account menu. No payment, no invoices.
- No testimonials, customer counts, logos, ratings or time-saved statistics. No email address or phone number. "Jadwalkan demo" goes to `/workspace`.

## Decisions

- Kantor Hukum monthly price is Rp1.549.000 so yearly billing saves 2 months on every paid plan.
- The calculator picks the cheapest eligible plan honestly. Shared cases (Kantor Hukum) and voice recognition (Pro and above) are explicit needs.
- The page renders without JavaScript and without providers. It is not prerendered because the language is negotiated per request.
