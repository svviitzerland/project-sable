# 2026-11-04: Interactive UI mockup for the workspace information architecture

## Scope

- **Goal.** Brainstorm a responsive, adaptive workspace around an information-architecture sketch: Case connects to Hearings, Findings and Evidence; Audio and Pasal.id feed Hearings (Pasal.id also feeds the case assistant); the case assistant reaches Hearings, Findings and Evidence; Hearings run Live or Recorded; Courtroom Practice is marked standalone / not needed; a Moodboard sits apart; the core objects are the case document, the hearing statement and the hearing finding.
- **Deliverable.** `design/mockup/`, a Svelte 5 + Vite prototype with every service simulated in the browser and synthetic data only. It is UI-only, separate from `apps/web`, and not deployed. Product scope, the API and deployment are unchanged, so `docs/product/PRODUCT_SPEC.md` is unchanged.
- **Feature inventory.** The `#/map` page lists, for each sketch node, what the product does today (from `PRODUCT.md`, `docs/product/PRODUCT_SPEC.md`, `DESIGN.md`, the i18n strings and the current workspace) and what the mockup proposes.

## Decisions the mockup proposes

- Four case sections (Overview, Hearings, Findings, Evidence) replace the seven case tabs, which overflow on phones today. Case documents move under Evidence, Legal references disappear as a tab, and settings/export go to the case menu.
- The case assistant becomes a context-aware layer (docked ≥1280px, drawer on tablets, sheet on phones) instead of a tab that loses context.
- Live and Recorded hearings are separate screens. A persistent live bar keeps a recording hearing visible elsewhere; ending it produces a review screen with a timeline of finding markers.
- Findings get a cross-hearing review queue.
- Courtroom Practice is left out of navigation.
- Night theme for courtrooms, density setting, and layout decided by container width.

## Round 12 (2026-11-05): Keterkaitan groups the evidence

- The user's direction moved in four steps: a 2D map for the overview with zoom to focus; then one chosen "driver" with the opposite kind around it (a tree that may repeat items); then choosing one thing should light up the component it belongs to; finally, the shape built here. Evidence is the umbrella term: anything that belongs to a case (a line said in a hearing, a document passage, a finding).
- The page is a pannable, zoomable map of all evidence by type: a column per hearing (its lines in order), one for findings, one per document (its passages in order). A pairing is a line in its relation's colour, solid once confirmed and dashed while Jude suggests it; evidence without a pairing stays a thin tick so each column keeps its shape. Zoom is semantic: far out a face and a time or a page, closer who or where and a line of text, close up two lines. Wheel or two fingers pan, Ctrl or a pinch zooms, the arrow keys move between pieces of evidence.
- Choosing any piece gathers its group: everything its pairings reach, directly or through other evidence. Pairings run both ways, so a group is a connected component; a document can span several groups. The rest fades, the group glides into place by kind (lines, findings, passages) as columns or rows, whichever this view shows larger, each row ordered by where its pairings point, and the camera follows. Esc or "Semua bukti" returns.
- Beside it, the group panel names the group (by its finding worth a look), lists its sources and its evidence by type, and its pairings: confirm Jude's suggestions, change a relation, take a pairing apart (undo in the toast; when a finding still cites the passage, the toast says so), or pair any member with other evidence, which can merge groups. What a finding pairs stays the finding's: findings are links into Temuan, consistent with Round 8. The ranked list for one subject and its trail are retired.
- Also: the camera is a small shared module (`panzoom.svelte.ts`), and the landing page now loads on demand, which keeps the main bundle under Vite's size warning after the map was added.
- Verification: `pnpm check` and `pnpm build` pass, the Impeccable detector reports no findings, the interaction checks (now 57, covering the type order, findings on the map, gathering a group, its name, members and Temuan links, confirming, relabelling, unpairing, undo, pairing that merges groups, Esc, and old links) and the viewer checks (12) pass, and the map and a group were captured at 1440x900 (including mid-transition) and 390x844 with no horizontal overflow or page errors.
