# 2026-11-04: Interactive UI mockup for the workspace information architecture

## Scope

- **Goal.** Brainstorm a responsive, adaptive workspace around an information-architecture sketch: Case connects to Hearings, Findings and Evidence; Audio and Pasal.id feed Hearings (Pasal.id also feeds the case assistant); the case assistant reaches Hearings, Findings and Evidence; Hearings run Live or Recorded; Courtroom Practice is marked standalone / not needed; a Moodboard sits apart; the core objects are the case document, the hearing statement and the hearing finding.
- **Deliverable.** `design/mockup/`, a Svelte 5 + Vite prototype with every service simulated in the browser and synthetic data only. It is UI-only, separate from `apps/web`, and not deployed. Product scope, the API and deployment are unchanged, so `docs/product/PRODUCT_SPEC.md` is unchanged.
- **Feature inventory.** The `#/map` page lists, for each sketch node, what the product does today (from `PRODUCT.md`, `docs/product/PRODUCT_SPEC.md`, `DESIGN.md`, the i18n strings and the current workspace) and what the mockup proposes.

## Decisions the mockup proposes

- Four case sections (Overview, Hearings, Findings, Evidence) replace the seven case tabs, which overflow on phones today. Case documents move under Evidence, Legal references disappear as a tab, and settings/export go to the case menu.
- The case assistant becomes a context-aware layer (docked ≥1280px, drawer on tablets, sheet on phones) instead of a tab that loses context.
- Live and Recorded hearings are separate screens. A persistent live bar keeps a recording hearing visible elsewhere; ending it produces a review screen with a timeline of finding markers.
- Findings get a cross-hearing review queue.
- Courtroom Practice is left out of navigation.
- Night theme for courtrooms, density setting, and layout decided by container width.

## Round 2: calmer screens and a user-driven board

- **Recording screen.** Less technical: no analysis phases or per-line timestamps; game-inspired speaker portraits with a voice ring, a visual-novel style dialogue box for the current line, and a who-spoke-when timeline in review.
- **Findings that matter.** Supported findings and findings without enough evidence stay quiet ("Others"); differences from a source and context-needed findings are shown with both phrases highlighted and a suggested question.
- **Evidence board.** The full evidence map gave way to a board the lawyer drives: choose a starting point, then pin only the suggestions you want.
- **Landing page.** A first animated landing page was added at `#/`; the workspace home moved to `#/cases`.

## Round 3: relevance first, the board in Findings, the original landing world

- **Live screen order.** The courtroom seating chart is gone. The screen now shows the finding most worth a look at the top (new arrivals first, then differences from a source, then the newest; "Later" moves one down), the person speaking now beneath it, then recent lines. Unnamed voices are identified from the dialogue box.
- **Board and Connections.** The board moved to Findings and gained finding cards. Evidence has a Connections view instead: pick one thing, see what relates to it ranked, follow any row, and step back along a trail.
- **Landing page.** Rebuilt inside `DESIGN.md`'s Editorial Case File world rather than a generic modern style: the original forest case-file photograph frames the opening, the ivory paper comparison rises from its lower edge, Rufina, Caslon Display and Manrope keep their roles, and OpenLaw's attorney page served as a pacing reference (an inset hero frame, generous spreads, product moments on material plates, scattered sources converging, a real-looking document page). Motion is one finite comparison sequence with replay, plus one gathering moment; both pause offscreen and respect reduced motion.
- **Impeccable.** The skill was installed locally (gitignored) and its context loaded. The user was offline, so its interview and decision page did not run and no concept roll was used; the direction came from the user's brief and `DESIGN.md`, and the build was code-led (no image generation). The detector flagged two accent-stripe borders; those and similar stripes and typed status glyphs were replaced. An independent finish review asked for eight fixes (the hero thread, Jude's note moved into the BAP page's margin, the record set in Manrope instead of Rufina, a sharp cloth tile instead of an upscaled photo, a synthetic label on the transcript, heading-first order on phones, the full suggested question on the live phone view, a sentence-case board tag); all were applied, and the plates and closing now use papers rising from the cloth's edge. The second pass scored six resolved. It reported the hero thread missing from the full-page capture (a capture artifact: Playwright's default full-page screenshot restarts the sequence, while real resizes do not; captures now settle motion first) and the live card's speaker attribution dropped by the batch (restored). Both were fixed after that pass and not re-reviewed, because two rounds is Impeccable's budget for an unattended run. The concept roll stays skipped by design. The direction is recorded in `.impeccable/surfaces/design-mockup-src-routes-landing-svelte.md`.
