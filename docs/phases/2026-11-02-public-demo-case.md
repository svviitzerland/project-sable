# 2026-11-02 — Real public demo case and Direktori Putusan MA ingestion

## Scope

- **Ingestion of Direktori Putusan MA PDFs** (`apps/web/src/lib/server/court-text.ts`, `documents.ts`). Tested with the real 696-page Putusan PN Jakarta Selatan 796/Pid.B/2022/PN Jkt.Sel (4.2 MB).
  - What broke: the 400,000-character limit rejected the decision (2.44 million raw characters; 1.66 million after cleanup) and the 200-page limit rejected it before that. PDF.js items were joined with a space after every item, splitting words at font runs ("p ada K antor H ukum", "202 2"). Each page carried the rotated "Direktori Putusan Mahkamah Agung Republik Indonesia" watermark as fragments ("ahkamah Agung Republ", "ik Indones"; in layout-mode text "ub", "ep", "R", "In"), the `putusan.mahkamahagung.go.id` header, "Hal. X dari 696 hal. Putusan Nomor ..." and a five-line disclaimer ending "Halaman X".
  - Fix: limits are now 25 MB, 1,500 pages and 3,000,000 cleaned characters (archives accept 25 MB originals; hearing uploads stay at 6 MB). Text items are joined by position. Rotated items that are fragments of the watermark phrase are dropped. When a document carries MA markers (any format), watermark-fragment lines, header/page lines and disclaimer lines are removed; PDF text gets whitespace normalized. The stored original is never modified. Chunking is unchanged (1,450-character windows within a page), and quote verification runs on the cleaned passages.
  - Result: full decision in about 1.8 s locally, 696 pages, 1,812 passages, peak RSS about 0.6–0.7 GB; keyword retrieval for Susi's opening testimony ranks page 202 first (33 ms). No residual "Disclaimer", URL or "Hal. X dari" lines.
  - Tests: `tests/court-text.test.ts` (synthetic layout noise, PDF.js header variants, gating on MA markers, position-aware joining) and a 70 KB real excerpt fixture `tests/fixtures/putusan-ma-excerpt.pdf` (pages 202–203, resource-pruned) that extracts cleanly and verifies a quote across a line break.
- **Transcription.** Amazon Transcribe batch job `jude-sambo-796-susi-kodir` (ap-southeast-1, `id-ID`, `ShowSpeakerLabels`, `MaxSpeakerLabels=6`) on the 72-minute 16 kHz mono WAV of the KOMPASTV broadcast (YouTube `tyhOkQ0q1-o`), the 8 November 2022 session of the Ferdy Sambo and Putri Candrawathi trial. Input was uploaded to `project-sable-ops-…/tmp/transcribe/`; job ran ~6.5 minutes; audio, output and the job were deleted afterwards (bucket is unversioned; no objects remain under `tmp/`).
- **Demo case** built through the app's own API and analysis code against a temporary SQLite directory with `AI_PROVIDER=bedrock`, exported as `.jude`, bundled at `apps/web/static/demo/sambo-796-public.jude` and in `examples/sambo-796-public/` with a bilingual README (sources, page map, limits).
- **Demo contents.** Document: 68-page excerpt (original pages 202–214: Susi, Diryanto alias Kodir, Damson, Alfonsius Dua Luren, Abdul Somad, Marjuki; 267–321: Richard Eliezer, Ricky Rizal, Kuat Ma'ruf), 512 KB, resource-pruned with pypdf (page content unchanged). ## ASR quality observed

- Output: 7,538 words, 595 audio segments, 5 speaker labels. Mean word confidence 0.75; 19% of words below 0.5. No word error rate was measured (no reference transcript exists).
- Content spot-check against the decision's summary of Susi's testimony (p. 202): the transcript matches on every checked fact: employed since 2020, first at Jalan Bangka, moved to Saguling in 2021, Kuat Ma'ruf had Covid-19, Ricky Rizal was in Magelang, Richard Eliezer at Saguling but "tidak menetap, kadang di posko", Diryanto works at Duren Tiga as a household assistant, Damson a security guard, Marjuki unknown to her.
- Proper nouns are the weakest part: "Qodir/kotir" (Kodir), "Ferdi", "Putri Crawati/Chandrawati", "Saguli/Suling/Sangguling" (Saguling), "Durian T/Doren 3" (Duren Tiga), "Om Jaswa/Yoswa" (Yosua), "Kuad", "suci" (Susi), "Sampo". Fillers ("ee") are transcribed; overlapping speech is lost.
- Diarization is unreliable: the prosecutor and Susi share one label for the first examination, the presiding judge and Kodir share another, and later one label covers a member judge, counsel and several witnesses. A Bedrock pass (`claude-sonnet-4-6`) split the 595 segments into 916 turns and proposed roles; every turn was accepted only if its words equal the ASR text (0 fallbacks). 15 filler-only turns were dropped and consecutive same-speaker turns merged into 684 turns, split at 35:15 into two hearings (358 and 326 turns) because a hearing holds at most 500 turns. The opening KUHAP Pasal 173 request is kept as `spk_0`; overall 80 of the 684 turns keep a `spk_N` label.

## Limits and concerns

- The original BAP is not public. The comparison source is the court's summary of testimony in the decision, which itself summarizes testimony from the Sambo trial; a "supported" finding means the transcript matches that summary, not an independent record.
- Role labels and turn boundaries were AI-proposed (Bedrock `claude-sonnet-4-6`) on top of Transcribe's diarization and were verified only for verbatim wording, not against the video. `spk_N` marks uncertain roles.
- Result: 97 findings (40 supported, 36 context needed, 21 insufficient evidence, 0 potential inconsistencies; one screened inconsistency was rejected by the independent reviewer). 245 witness turns were not checkable; 17 still failed after retries. The court's summary comes from this trial, so agreement is expected; documented differences concern the unpublished BAP.
- The demo analysis used Bedrock `claude-haiku-4-5` for screening and `claude-sonnet-4-6` for the independent review (failed turns retried with the two swapped), overriding the production default (`amazon.nova-lite`), because a trial run with Nova Lite cited unrelated passages for non-assertions (e.g. a judge's procedural question marked "supported"). Only witness turns (`Saksi …` and uncertain `spk_N`) were analyzed; bench and counsel turns are kept without findings.
- Haiku 4.5 omitted the required `verification` field in roughly a third of first attempts ("The AI response could not be verified"), and some explanations came back in English. Many context-needed findings are weak.
- The account's Bedrock quota for these Anthropic models is 10 requests per minute per model. Production analysis at hearing pace with three concurrent statements will throttle if switched to these models without a quota increase.
- Names of the defendants' children that occurred in the transcript (two mentions) are replaced with "[nama anak disamarkan]". The decision itself names one child once (original p. 206); the excerpt keeps the court's text unchanged, so that name appears in one source passage and one supported finding's quote. Remove or redact before wider distribution if required. The decision's rejected sexual-assault allegations are present only as the decision and broadcast state them.
- Three copies of the excerpt data are committed (static archive, example archive, example PDF; under 2 MB in total). The 696-page PDF and the audio are not committed.
