AUTHOR'S DISCUSSION GUIDE — NOT EVIDENCE; DO NOT UPLOAD TO THE CASE
================================================================

This is a fictional exercise with deliberately incomplete records. These are useful
review targets, not guaranteed AI outputs or an adjudicated account of what happened.
All people and events are invented. Case reference: TRAINING-CW-014.

1. ARRIVAL TIME: POTENTIAL INCONSISTENCY
   H01 turn 3 places Raka's first arrival at 21:30; D01 records his earlier estimate
   of about 20:10 from his phone. D02 records DR-17 at 20:08:41. The earlier statement
   and gate event are close in time, but a card assignment does not prove its holder.
   H01 turn 5 explicitly introduces uncertainty rather than resolving the discrepancy.
   Ask which clock he used, whether there were multiple visits and whether the full
   interview or additional contemporaneous records can resolve the difference.

2. CCTV CLOCK: CONTEXT, NOT A SECOND VISIT
   D03 reports a +64-minute display offset. Displayed 21:12 minus 64 minutes is about
   20:08 WIB. That may reconcile the CCTV description with D02. It does NOT reconcile
   a claimed 21:30 first arrival automatically, identify a face or establish contents.
   The offset's stability and incomplete export require independent verification.
   The described image and report are fictional; no actual video is included.

3. AUTHORIZATION: POTENTIAL INCONSISTENCY
   H01 turn 7 says cartons of tablets were authorized. D04 expressly authorizes only
   empty packaging; D06 says not to take stock from T-3. This challenges the stated
   authorization, but does not prove what was loaded, intent or a theft.

4. CARTON CONTENTS: PARTLY SUPPORTED; KEY GAP REMAINS
   H01 turn 9 says Raka did not open the cartons or receive contents confirmation.
   D01 gives a matching account. D06 shows him asking who checked the cartons, and
   Maya saying she cannot confirm. D04's contents-verification field is blank.
   These records are compatible, but selected messages are not a complete export.

5. GUARD CONTINUITY: POTENTIAL INCONSISTENCY
   H01 turn 11 says Dimas never left the gate. D02 records a 20:20–20:45 inspection;
   D07 and H01 turn 13 describe the patrol. Dimas's stated 20:34 observation is not
   independently confirmed by an included patrol note. Do not invent that exhibit.
   Whether Raka could observe the gate for the whole period remains uncertain.

6. STOCK COUNT: SUPPORT FOR A DIFFERENCE, NOT FOR A PERPETRATOR
   H01 turn 19 matches D05: 20 tablets minus 8 equals a 12-unit discrepancy discovered
   at 08:15 the following morning. There is no documented 20:30 count. The removal
   time, overnight access, serial numbers and possible internal movement are unknown.
   Two cartons are not equivalent to twelve devices. No supplied source proves that
   the cartons contained the missing tablets or that Raka moved tablets from T-3.

7. RETURNS RECEIPT: INSUFFICIENT EVIDENCE
   H01 turn 23 asserts a 09:00 next-morning receipt. No such receipt is supplied.
   Ask for it and verify its provenance. Its absence from this pack is not proof
   that delivery did not happen. D04's blank acknowledgement concerns the release
   form and is not itself a completed returns-desk delivery receipt.

8. WEATHER: A SMALL CORROBORATING DETAIL
   D02 records rain from 18:55 through 21:00. D01 refers to a rain jacket. H01 turn 25
   says it was raining before arrival. That is compatible with the recorded weather,
   but weather does not resolve which arrival time is right or establish identity.

WHAT A USEFUL ASSISTANT SHOULD PRESERVE
   - Source attribution, limitations and uncertainty, not just a neat single timeline.
   - The distinction between a witness claim, an instrument record and an inference.
   - The distinction between authorization, actual contents and later delivery.
   - No invented receipt, footage, seal number, biometric identification or confession.
   - No fabricated Indonesian statute, legal element, credibility score or guilty verdict.

SUGGESTED FOLLOW-UP REQUESTS
   Original complete CCTV and recorder logs; clock calibration evidence; original
   message export; packing inspection/seal records; returns-desk receipt; complete
   overnight access logs; inventory scan history and serial-number reconciliation.

No analysis findings are preloaded. Different model outputs may be reasonable if
they accurately quote sources and preserve limitations. Treat this as a functional
practice pack, not a validated benchmark or a measure of legal/AI accuracy.
