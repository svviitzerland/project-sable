JUDE EXAMPLE CASE: CENDANA WAREHOUSE
=================================

Entirely fictional training material. All names, organizations and events are invented.
No real court documents, personal data, statutory quotations, audio or video are included.
English-language materials; invented Indonesian setting. Incident wall-clock times use WIB (UTC+7).

QUICKEST START — IMPORT THE COMPLETE CASE

1. Sign in to Jude at /workspace.
2. Use "Import .jude" in the case navigation.
3. Select Cendana-warehouse-complete.jude (NOT the outer ZIP).
4. Open the imported case, "SYNTHETIC — Cendana warehouse loss".

The archive includes 8 source documents, their originals and searchable passages,
one 26-turn hearing, and 4 authored evidence connections. The connections start
unreviewed. Inspect their quotations before confirming them. There are no prewritten
AI findings, embeddings, account credentials or conversations in the archive.

MANUAL UPLOAD — TRY THE DOCUMENT AND HEARING IMPORT FLOWS

Use a separate new case if you have already imported the complete archive.
Suggested title: SYNTHETIC — Cendana warehouse loss.
Suggested reference: TRAINING-CW-014.

1. Upload the 8 files under documents/ to Case documents. There are two text-based
   PDFs, one DOCX and five TXT files. Their D01–D08 labels identify source exhibits.
2. Open Hearings and import hearings/H01-witness-examination.json. Review the preview,
   then confirm. H01 also comes as CSV, speaker-labeled TXT and a hearing-only .jude.
   Choose ONE version: these formats contain the same hearing.
3. For the AI formatter, try H02-unstructured-for-AI-conversion.txt in a separate
   hearing or separate test case. It intentionally requires "Convert with AI" and
   contains a shortened alternative to H01, not an additional independent witness.
   Check that the formatter preserves the source wording and uncertainty.

Hearing start/end values are invented relative playback positions for the exercise.
They are not actual recording timestamps and are distinct from incident times stated
in testimony. The TXT variant has turn start times only; JSON/CSV/.jude include ends.
No audio recording is supplied.

THINGS TO TRY

Evidence search:
  - "64 minutes" or "DR-17" for keyword search without AI.
  - "Which records help establish when the driver arrived?"
  - "Was taking tablets authorized, or only empty packaging?"

Evidence graph:
  - Inspect the four imported connections and their exact source quotes.
  - Confirm a connection only after checking its sources. Imported connections are
    authored exercise suggestions, not independently discovered or verified facts.
  - Use Find connections with OpenRouter configured to index passages and suggest
    further relationships. Do not equate a relationship with proof of guilt.

Hearings:
  - Read Raka's arrival account beside D01 and D02.
  - Use Analyze hearing when the analysis model and hosted job delivery are configured.
  - Review quotations, alternative explanations and missing context in each finding.
  - Analysis is an explicit action and uses model/queue quota. The 26 turns include
    questions and procedural remarks, not just factual assertions.

Case assistant:
  - "Build a timeline. Separate witness estimates from machine timestamps and cite sources."
  - "How does the CCTV clock error affect the arrival-time inconsistency?"
  - "What evidence is missing before we can connect the tablets to the cartons?"
  - "Prepare five non-leading questions for the security guard. Cite their factual basis."
  - "Which Indonesian legal issues need research? Do not invent statute text or a charge."
    Legal discovery, when configured, supplies links/metadata that need independent review.

Courtroom practice:
  - Role: defense counsel. Stage: witness examination.
  - Setting: fictional Indonesian criminal-hearing exercise; English language.
  - Goal: distinguish personal observations from assumptions and test timing/authorization.
  - Manually review any AI-suggested trial conditions. This pack establishes no charge,
    applicable statutory elements or admissibility ruling.
  - Ask for a debrief on questions and gaps, not a verdict or credibility score.

PROVIDER EXPECTATIONS

File import, source inspection and keyword search work without an AI provider.
Natural-language semantic retrieval, AI conversion, analysis and generative answers
need the relevant OpenRouter configuration. Hosted analysis also needs working QStash.
Without those providers, the sample still imports but does not magically create AI output.

KEEP THE EXERCISE NOTES OUT OF THE EVIDENCE

Do not upload START-HERE.txt or REVIEWER-NOTES.txt as evidence. The latter contains
discussion targets and suggested questions, not a source document. Uploading it would
contaminate retrieval with the author's answer guide. The complete .jude excludes both.

The outer ZIP is a download bundle. Extract it before selecting files in Jude.
Use a fresh case to compare alternate import formats and avoid duplicating testimony.
