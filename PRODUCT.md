# Jude

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Lawyers working on Indonesian criminal proceedings. The initial product scenario is a simulated hearing using synthetic or appropriately authorized documents and audio. Future prosecutor and judge roles are outside the initial scope.

## Product Purpose

Connect courtroom statements with case-specific evidence and relevant Indonesian legal references so lawyers can independently examine potential inconsistencies and prepare better-informed questions.

## Positioning

An evidence-grounded courtroom copilot that continuously connects testimony to source material, rather than requiring a lawyer to repeatedly supply context to a general-purpose chatbot.

## Operating Context

Lawyers listen to testimony while consulting investigation reports, witness statements, evidence, and legislation. Source provenance, uncertainty, and human judgment must remain explicit.

## Capabilities and Constraints

The supplied brief proposes live transcription and speaker attribution, contextual claim extraction, case-filtered retrieval, legal-reference lookup, bounded adversarial review, and progressive findings. These are intended MVP capabilities, not verified production integrations. Findings use Supported, Potential Inconsistency, Context Needed, and Insufficient Evidence statuses. Never equate an inconsistency with dishonesty or a finding with guilt.

The current deliverable is a marketing website introducing Jude, confirmed by the user. Build in the existing Next.js repository. Use a clearly labeled synthetic example; do not imply working courtroom audio, legal retrieval, authentication, or evidence storage on the marketing site.

## Brand Commitments

Name: Jude. The user approved a calm editorial visual direction using warm paper, deep forest green, large serif headlines, and a source-linked testimony example. Voice: precise, composed, useful to legal professionals.

## Product Principles

- Trace substantive findings back to inspectable sources.
- Distinguish direct support, possible inconsistencies, and unresolved context.
- Keep legal decisions with responsible professionals.
- Preserve attention during proceedings.
